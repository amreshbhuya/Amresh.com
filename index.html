Amresh Bhuyan is an Indian full-stack software developer, technology entrepreneur and engineer, widely recognized for his leadership in software development and for modernizing academic and enterprise management systems through technology.

As the Founder, Director and Chief Executive Officer of AediaX Tech Private Limited (established 6 June 2025), he focuses on building AI-driven platforms, scalable web applications and cross-platform mobile solutions — blending strategic thinking with hands-on engineering to deliver future-ready digital systems.

He is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering. Alongside engineering, he holds a strong interdisciplinary interest in biological systems and analytical problem-solving, which shapes his approach to software architecture, artificial intelligence and system design.

diversity_3

Amresh comes from a highly educated and entrepreneurial family. His father, Mr. Sanat Kumar Bhuyan (MBA), serves as Director at AediaX Tech, and his mother, Mrs. Puspalata Bhuyan (PG, Economics & Management), works as Accountant at AediaX. The family has also managed Bhuyan Warehouse in Baripada, Odisha for over three decades — a foundation that continues to shape his leadership discipline and long-term vision.
